Our Responsibility

Growing Better,
Not Just More

We treat the land as a partner, not a resource. Every decision — from irrigation to packaging — is weighed against its environmental cost.

Water Recycling

95% of our processing water is captured, filtered, and returned to the orchards — a closed loop that conserves over 2 million litres annually.

Solar Energy

Our 18,000m² rooftop solar array covers 80% of our facility's energy needs, cutting 4,200 tonnes of CO₂ per year.

Zero-Waste Policy

Apple pomace becomes organic fertiliser. Packaging scraps are collected and recycled. We target zero landfill output by 2027.

2015

Solar installation begins

Phase one of our renewable energy investment — 6,000m² of rooftop panels.

2018

Water recycling system live

Closed-loop water treatment plant operational. 95% reuse rate achieved within 12 months.

2021

Zero-waste commitment signed

Pomace-to-compost programme launched. Landfill output reduced by 78% in year one.

2024

Full solar expansion complete

Facility now generates 110% of daytime energy needs. Surplus feeds back to the national grid.

2027

Zero landfill target

Final milestone: complete elimination of landfill waste from all operations.
